Benefits Policy

The company provides a comprehensive benefits package to all full-time employees.

Eligible full-time employees receive:

- Medical health insurance
- Dental insurance
- Vision insurance
- Life insurance
- Access to employee assistance programs

Health insurance coverage becomes effective on the first day of the month following employment eligibility requirements.

Part-time employees may not be eligible for all benefit programs.

Employees should contact Human Resources with questions regarding benefits enrollment, coverage, or eligibility.
