Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

The company provides paid time off to eligible full-time employees.

Full-time employees receive 20 PTO days per calendar year.

PTO may be used for vacation, personal business, illness, or other approved absences.

Employees should submit PTO requests through the company's timekeeping system and obtain manager approval whenever possible.

Unused PTO will be handled according to applicable company policy and local regulations.

Questions regarding PTO balances and requests should be directed to Human Resources.
